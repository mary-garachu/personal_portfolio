The problem

A safari is a big, considered purchase, and the options multiply quickly: destinations, activities, trip types, trip lengths and price points. A visitor who can’t narrow that down fast tends to leave, or sends a vague enquiry that takes several emails to untangle.

So the site’s first job is to help a traveller get from “I want to go on safari” to a shortlist of real trips.

What I built

What the live site does today:

  • A trip search right in the home-page hero, filtering by destination, activities, duration and budget.
  • Navigation organised the way travellers think about trips: Trips, Activities, Destinations and Trip Types.
  • A wishlist, so visitors can save trips while they compare.
  • A contact email in the header (“Have questions?”) on every page.
  • A custom WordPress theme built on Understrap, a Bootstrap-based starter theme.
