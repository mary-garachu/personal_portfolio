The problem

Consultancies sell expertise you can’t see. Spirealm works with health organisations on system-level problems, so its site has to establish credibility quickly: who it is, who it serves, and what it stands for — before a visitor ever reaches a services list.

What I built

What the site showed (from the screenshot above):

  • A home-page hero that states the category (“Healthcare Consultancy”) and the full company name, with a single “Learn more” call to action.
  • A short four-item navigation: Home, About Us, Services, Contact.
  • A values section with icon-led items such as Innovation and Integrity, each with a one-line explanation.
  • A mission statement: “Enabling health organizations to overcome challenges by delivering impactful, sustainable, and expert-driven solutions.”
