The problem

The Ezra Group is a holding company for nine subsidiaries working across oil and gas, construction, power and energy, and real estate. That is a structure problem before it is a design problem: a visitor needs to understand the group as a whole, then find the part of it that is relevant to them.

What I built

What the site showed (from the screenshot above):

  • A full-width hero carousel with one slide per core sector — Energy Generation & Distribution, Infrastructure Construction, Real Estate Development — and labelled tabs to move between them.
  • Navigation that gives the group’s structure its own place: Home, About, Subsidiaries, Projects, CSR, News, Contact.
  • A “Who we are” introduction (“Building a Legacy Together”) explaining the group and its subsidiaries.
